Third-order polynomial baseline invasive-incidence and exponential non-breast-cancer mortality, both by race/ethnicity, combined by a year-by-year competing-risk recursion into a race-specific 5-year risk; that risk is then standardized to the BI-RADS-2 reference density using age- and race-specific adjustment factors, multiplied by the woman's own density category's relative hazard, and (if known) by simple family-history and breast-biopsy multipliers.
I(x,r) = a_r·x³+b_r·x²+c_r·x+d_r   (annual incidence, % )
  D(x,r) = n_r·e^(m_r·x)      (annual non-BC mortality, % )
  P(x,r): P(a,r)=1; P(x,r) = P(x-1,r) − I(x-1,r) − D(x-1,r)
  AR_r = Σ_{x=a}^{a+4} I(x,r)·P(x,r)              (race-specific 5y risk)

  risk = AR_r × adjustment[r,age_band] × density_RH[age<65][density]
         × family_history_mult (if known) × biopsy_mult (if known)
